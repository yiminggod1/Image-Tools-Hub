WebP vs JPG: Which Should You Use?

A practical comparison of WebP and JPG for websites, photos, and compatibility.

The practical approach

WebP is a modern image format that can offer smaller files for many web images, while JPG remains widely compatible. The right choice depends on the browsers, platforms, or downstream tools that must accept the file.

What to do

Use WebP when your destination supports it and file size matters. Use JPG when compatibility with older software or services matters.
