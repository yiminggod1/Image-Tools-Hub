How to Resize an Image Without Losing Quality

A guide to dimensions, interpolation, output format, and sensible downscaling.

The practical approach

Downscaling is usually safer than enlarging because reducing dimensions can discard pixels while enlarging requires creating new ones. Preserve the aspect ratio unless you intentionally need a stretched image.

What to do

For a photo, set one dimension and keep the ratio locked. Choose an output format appropriate to the destination.
