How to Convert PNG to JPG

When converting PNG graphics or screenshots to JPG makes sense, and what to check before exporting.

Know what changes

PNG supports lossless compression and transparency. JPG is designed around photographic compression and does not preserve transparency.

Use JPG for photographs

Photos, scans, and other continuous-tone images are often good candidates for JPG when smaller files matter. Keep PNG when crisp transparency or flat graphic edges are important.

Check the result

After conversion, inspect text edges, gradients, and any transparent areas. A transparent PNG needs a background color when exported to JPG.
