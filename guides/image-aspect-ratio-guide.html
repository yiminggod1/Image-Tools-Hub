Image Aspect Ratio Explained

How to calculate, preserve, and change an image's aspect ratio.

The practical approach

Aspect ratio is width divided by height. Common examples include 1:1, 4:3, and 16:9. Changing one dimension while keeping the ratio prevents unwanted stretching.

What to do

Calculate the ratio from the original pixels first. Then choose a new width or height and derive the other dimension from the same ratio.
