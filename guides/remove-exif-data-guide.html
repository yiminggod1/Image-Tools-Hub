What Is EXIF Data and How to Remove It

What image metadata can contain and why stripping it can help before sharing.

The practical approach

EXIF metadata can include camera information, timestamps, lens settings, orientation, and sometimes location data. Browser access to metadata varies by format, so a reliable privacy workflow is often to create a fresh exported image and check what remains.

What to do

When privacy matters, share a re-encoded copy rather than the original camera file.
