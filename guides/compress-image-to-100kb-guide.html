How to Compress an Image to 100KB

A practical guide to getting an image under a 100KB upload limit.

The practical approach

A file-size limit is a hard ceiling, so the safest workflow is to export as JPG or WebP, lower quality gradually, and confirm the final byte size after export. A 100KB target is 102,400 bytes in binary units. Very detailed photos may need stronger compression or smaller dimensions to reach the limit.

What to do

Keep the original file. Try the target-size tool first. If the image becomes visibly soft, reduce the pixel dimensions and then compress again.
