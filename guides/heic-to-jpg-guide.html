How to Convert HEIC Photos to JPG

Why iPhone photos may use HEIC and when JPG is more convenient.

The practical approach

HEIC is common on Apple devices because it can store photos efficiently. JPG is still easier to share with many websites, forms, desktop apps, and older services. Conversion changes the container/encoding; it does not magically restore detail that was not captured in the original photo.

What to do

Keep the HEIC original as an archive and convert a copy to JPG when compatibility is the goal.
