How to Reduce an Image to 1MB

A practical workflow for getting a photo or graphic below a 1MB file-size limit.

Start with the destination limit

1MB is a file-size ceiling, not a quality setting. Keep the original, create a working copy, and verify the exported file size before you submit or upload it.

Try format and quality first

For photographs, JPG or WebP usually gives you more control over bytes than a lossless PNG export. Lower quality gradually and stop when the image still looks acceptable for its destination.

Reduce dimensions when quality alone is not enough

A very large image can remain heavy even at strong compression. Reducing pixel dimensions lowers the amount of image data that has to be encoded.
